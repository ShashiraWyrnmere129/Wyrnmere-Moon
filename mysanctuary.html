Deploying Your Owner-Only Route

1. Host as a Private Page on Your Website

Save this exact file as owner-portal.html (or /sanctuary.html) in your website's root folder alongside your index.html. Do not link to it in your public navigation bar—bookmark the direct URL for your private access.

2. Add a Subtle Footer Moon Trigger (Optional)

Place a discreet crescent moon icon in your website's footer that navigates to your owner portal:

<a href="/owner-portal.html" aria-label="Owner Sanctuary" style="opacity:0.4;">☾</a>

3. Upgrading to Multi-Device Cloud Sync

Right now, your data is saved instantly in your browser's localStorage (use Download JSON Backup anytime to transfer between phone & laptop). When you are ready for real-time database sync across all devices, connect this file to Supabase or Firebase Auth + Firestore so your PIN is verified server-side.

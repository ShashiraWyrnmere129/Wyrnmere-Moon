The Story Behind The Wyrnmere Moon

The Wyrnmere Moon was born from a lifelong passion for spirituality, intuition and helping others find clarity along their journey.

With over 20 years of experience exploring tarot, oracle readings, palmistry and spiritual practices, I have developed a personal approach rooted in compassion, curiosity and reflection.

My journey has been one of learning, growth and discovering the importance of listening to intuition. I believe every person deserves a welcoming space where they can explore their questions without judgment.

My Philosophy

I believe readings should empower, not frighten. They are opportunities to reflect, consider possibilities and reconnect with your own inner strength.

No reading can replace your own choices or determine your future. Your path belongs to you.

Why Wyrnmere Moon?

The moon represents cycles, transformation, mystery and renewal. It reminds us that even through darkness, light continues to exist.

Wyrnmere Moon is more than a business. It is a passion transformed into a purpose: helping others find perspective, embrace intuition and recognize their own strength.

"Sometimes all we need is a little light when the path ahead feels uncertain."

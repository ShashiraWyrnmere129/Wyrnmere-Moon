Our Story

Welcome to Wyrnmere Moon

Wyrnmere Moon was born from a lifelong passion for spirituality, intuition, and helping others find clarity along their journey.

With over 20 years of experience in tarot, oracle readings, palmistry, and spiritual practices, I offer a welcoming space for self-discovery, reflection, and guidance.

A Journey of Intuition

Every person's journey is different. Sometimes we need a fresh perspective, a moment of reflection, or simply a place where we can explore the questions on our hearts without judgment.

My approach is compassionate, thoughtful, and centered on helping people recognize their own inner strength.

Our Philosophy

The moon represents cycles, transformation, renewal, and the beauty of finding light during uncertain moments.

At Wyrnmere Moon, readings are intended for insight, reflection, and personal exploration. Your choices and your journey remain your own.

"Sometimes all we need is a little light when the path ahead feels uncertain."
